# Contributor Agreement — Template (IP Assignment + Confidentiality)

> **⚠️ This is a plain-English starting template, NOT legal advice.**
> It was prepared to save drafting time only. Before anyone signs it, have it
> reviewed and finalized by a qualified attorney licensed in your
> jurisdiction (Washington State / USA). Laws on work-for-hire, IP
> assignment, minors' data, and contractor vs. volunteer status vary, and an
> unreviewed form may not protect Medina Academy. Fill in every **[bracketed]**
> field.

---

This Contributor Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between:

- **Owner:** Medina Academy, operating the "MedinaCares Volunteer Service
  Awards" platform, located at [address] ("the Owner"); and
- **Contributor:** [full legal name], located at [address] ("the
  Contributor").

Effective date: **[date]**.

## 1. Purpose
The Contributor will provide software development, design, and/or related
services (the "Work") for the Owner's MedinaCares Volunteer Service Awards
platform and related materials (the "Project").

## 2. Assignment of intellectual property
The Contributor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Owner all right, title, and
interest worldwide in and to the Work and anything created in connection with
the Project, including all source code, designs, documentation, and all
copyrights, patents, trademarks, and trade-secret rights. To the extent any
part of the Work qualifies as a "work made for hire," it is owned by the Owner
as such; to the extent it does not, it is assigned to the Owner by this
Agreement. The Contributor will sign any further documents the Owner
reasonably needs to record or perfect this ownership.

## 3. Moral rights and prior materials
The Contributor waives any moral rights in the Work to the extent permitted by
law. If the Contributor includes any pre-existing materials they own, the
Contributor grants the Owner a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to
use them within the Project, and warrants they have the right to do so.

## 4. No third-party or infringing material
The Contributor warrants that the Work is original, does not infringe any
third party's rights, and does not include code or assets whose license would
impose obligations on the Owner (for example, copyleft licenses) without the
Owner's prior written approval.

## 5. Confidentiality
The Contributor will keep confidential all non-public information of the
Owner, including source code, credentials, business information, and any
personal data accessed through the Project ("Confidential Information"), and
will use it only to perform the Work. These obligations continue after this
Agreement ends. On request or on termination, the Contributor will return or
delete all Confidential Information and copies.

## 6. Personal data and minors
The Project processes personal data of minors. The Contributor will:
- access only the data strictly necessary for the Work, and only in
  environments the Owner authorizes;
- **never** access, copy, export, or transmit production/live data, and work
  only with test data unless the Owner gives specific written authorization;
- follow all applicable privacy and data-protection laws (including, where
  applicable, COPPA and FERPA) and the Owner's instructions;
- promptly notify the Owner of any suspected data incident; and
- not use any personal data for any purpose outside the Work.

## 7. Security
The Contributor will protect credentials, use only Owner-approved services,
not introduce malware or unauthorized data collection, and not deploy to
production. Production deployment and credentials remain solely with the
Owner.

## 8. Independent contractor
The Contributor is an independent contractor (or volunteer), not an employee,
partner, or agent of the Owner, and is responsible for their own taxes. This
Agreement creates no employment relationship.

## 9. Compensation
[Describe payment, if any — e.g. "$[amount] per [hour/milestone]", or
"volunteer, no compensation". If a volunteer, confirm they still agree to the
IP assignment in Section 2.]

## 10. Term and termination
Either party may end this Agreement with [notice period] written notice.
Sections 2–7 survive termination. On termination the Contributor's access is
revoked.

## 11. No conflicting obligations
The Contributor represents that signing this Agreement and doing the Work does
not breach any other agreement they have (for example, an employer's IP or
non-compete terms).

## 12. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington, USA. It is
the entire agreement on this subject and replaces any prior understanding. If
any part is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
