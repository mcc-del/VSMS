MedinaCares Volunteer Service Awards

Nonprofit Partner Pre-Approval Application

Thank you for offering volunteer opportunities to MedinaCares students. Please complete this application and email it to mcc@medinaacademy.org. Once approved, your organization will appear in the app so students can log verified service hours with you.

Approval criteria

To be approved, an organization should:
  • Be a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with a valid EIN (tax ID).
  • Provide ample, ongoing volunteer opportunities appropriate for students (grades 2–12).
  • Offer ease of access to sign up — through your own website/portal, or by posting opportunities on the MedinaCares platform.
  • Provide adult supervision on site and be willing to verify and sign off on students' hours.
  • Maintain a safe environment for minors (e.g. supervision policy; no unsupervised one-on-one contact between a student and an unrelated adult).
  • Have a responsive contact who can confirm student participation when asked.
  • Serve the local community or be reasonably accessible to students.
