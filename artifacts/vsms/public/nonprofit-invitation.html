MedinaCares Volunteer Service Awards

Invitation to Become an Approved Nonprofit Partner

Dear Nonprofit Partner,

A MedinaCares student would like to volunteer with your organization and have those hours count toward our Volunteer Service Awards. We invite your organization to become an approved partner so students can log verified service hours with you.

What we look for in a partner

To be approved, an organization should:

  • Be a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with a valid EIN (tax ID).
  • Provide ample, ongoing volunteer opportunities appropriate for students (grades 2–12).
  • Offer easy sign-up — through your own website/portal, or by posting opportunities on the MedinaCares platform.
  • Provide adult supervision on site and be willing to verify and sign off on students' hours.
  • Maintain a safe environment for minors.
  • Have a responsive contact who can confirm student participation.

How to apply

If your organization meets the above, please email us and simply tell us how you meet each requirement. Include:

  1. Organization legal name and EIN
  2. Website and a primary contact (name, email, phone)
  3. The kinds of volunteer opportunities you offer students, and how they sign up
  4. A brief note on supervision and how you'll verify hours
Email your response to mcc@medinaacademy.org. Once reviewed and approved, your organization will appear in our app for students to select.

With gratitude,
The MedinaCares Council
